01 / WHY WE BUILD

Because the
web is still full of possibility.

We build because we're curious. We like figuring things out, turning rough ideas into real experiences, and paying attention to the details that make something feel right.

Our work can take different forms — websites, documentation, tools, or experiments that teach us something new. We value clarity over noise and useful work over big claims.

02 / WHAT GUIDES US

Our working principles.

Simple ideas that shape how we approach the work.

01

Stay curious.

Ask better questions. Try things. Keep learning.

02

Care about craft.

Make the details count, from the first sketch to the final click.

03

Keep it human.

Technology should be understandable, welcoming, and useful.

04

Share the process.

When possible, leave the door open for others to learn and build.

03 / OPEN SOURCE

Open by choice.
Transparent by default.

We aim to publish source code, documentation, and useful project context whenever we have the right to share them. Open repositories make it easier to inspect how something works, learn from the decisions, report issues, and build on ideas.

Some things need a boundary. Personal data, security-sensitive details, third-party licensing, infrastructure, and project commitments can make parts of a project unsuitable for public release. We will not label something open source unless its license and permissions support that claim. We would rather explain a limitation than make an empty promise.
